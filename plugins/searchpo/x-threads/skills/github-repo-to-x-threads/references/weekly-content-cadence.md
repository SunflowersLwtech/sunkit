# Weekly Content Cadence

Use this when the user asks for account strategy, scheduling, or content system
planning.

## Default Cadence

- 2 technical threads: paper-to-system or repo-to-system.
- 1 build-in-public note: one failure, one fix, one next validation.
- 1 short question: a concrete tradeoff for Agent builders.
- 1 reusable visual/asset: checklist, flowchart, prompt, issue template, or
  before/after table.

## Creator Pattern Mix

Keep the week balanced across content modes:

- 1 `engineering_mentor` post: product/paper/repo judgment with a practical
  rule.
- 1 `demo_first_tool` post: real tool use, screenshot/video when available, and
  a limitation.
- 1 `builder_resource_log` post: progress, numbered resources, next validation,
  and community loop.
- 1 `product_translator_longform` post when there is a strong source: paper,
  podcast, product case, or English tool/resource translated into product
  implications and a reading path.
- Optional `test_catalog` short posts only when the update is timely and the
  status boundary is clear.

## Quality Bar

Do not increase volume if quality gates fail. A week with two strong posts is
better than seven mixed posts that blur the account.

## Active Engagement

For each thread, identify 5-8 relevant builders, maintainers, researchers, or
projects to mention only when genuinely relevant. Do not tag unrelated accounts
for reach.

## Main Account Filter

Ask before publishing:

- Does this teach something about Agent reliability or embodied AI systems?
- Would a future collaborator understand what I build from this post?
- Is there a reusable asset?
- Is there one concrete discussion question?
- Is emotion converted into evidence, failure mode, or design lesson?
- Is the creator pattern coherent, and are demo/test/news claims truthfully
  bounded?
