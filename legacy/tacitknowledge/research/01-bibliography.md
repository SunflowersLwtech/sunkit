# Michael Polanyi & Tacit Knowledge: Complete Bibliography

---

## 1. Michael Polanyi's Own Works

### Major Books

**Personal Knowledge: Towards a Post-Critical Philosophy**
- Year: 1958 (corrected edition 1962)
- Publisher: Routledge & Kegan Paul (UK); University of Chicago Press (US)
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67288-5
- Polanyi's magnum opus arguing that all knowledge is personal and involves the knower's passionate participation. Develops the concept of tacit knowing as foundational to all explicit knowledge, challenging the ideal of scientific detachment.

**The Tacit Dimension**
- Year: 1966
- Publisher: Doubleday & Company (US); Routledge & Kegan Paul (UK). Reissued by University of Chicago Press (2009).
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67298-4 (Chicago reissue)
- Based on his 1962 Terry Lectures at Yale. "We can know more than we can tell." Elaborates the from-to structure of tacit knowing, with implications for science, art, and society.

**Science, Faith and Society**
- Year: 1946 (revised edition 1964)
- Publisher: Oxford University Press (1946); University of Chicago Press (1964)
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67290-8
- Based on his 1946 Riddell Memorial Lectures. Science rests on a community of trust and shared commitments rather than on a purely objective method.

**The Study of Man**
- Year: 1959
- Publisher: University of Chicago Press; Routledge & Kegan Paul
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67296-0
- Based on his 1958 Lindsay Memorial Lectures. A compact introduction to his epistemology.

**The Logic of Liberty: Reflections and Rejoinders**
- Year: 1951
- Publisher: Routledge & Kegan Paul; University of Chicago Press
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67296-0
- Essays on the social conditions of free thought. Critiques central planning and defends spontaneous coordination.

**Knowing and Being: Essays by Michael Polanyi**
- Year: 1969
- Publisher: University of Chicago Press; Routledge & Kegan Paul
- Editor: Marjorie Grene
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67285-4
- Key essays spanning 1959-1968. Covers tacit knowing, the structure of consciousness, the body-mind problem, and the hierarchy of levels in nature.

**Meaning**
- Year: 1975
- Publisher: University of Chicago Press
- Co-author: Harry Prosch
- ISBN: 978-0-226-67294-6
- Final philosophical statement. Extends tacit knowing into art, myth, religion, and meaning.

**The Contempt of Freedom: The Russian Experiment and After**
- Year: 1940
- Publisher: Watts & Co. (London)
- Early political work on Soviet economic planning and totalitarianism threatening intellectual freedom.

**Full Employment and Free Trade**
- Year: 1945
- Publisher: Cambridge University Press
- ISBN: 978-1-107-68209-1 (2005 reissue)
- Polanyi's contribution to economics, independently developing ideas parallel to Keynesian economics.

### Key Papers and Lectures

- **"The Stability of Beliefs"** (1952) — British Journal for the Philosophy of Science
- **"Skills and Connoisseurship"** (1958) — Atti del Congresso di Studi Metodologici
- **"The Unaccountable Element in Science"** (1962) — Philosophy, Vol. 37
- **"Tacit Knowing: Its Bearing on Some Problems of Philosophy"** (1962) — Reviews of Modern Physics, Vol. 34
- **"The Logic of Tacit Inference"** (1966) — Philosophy, Vol. 41
- **"Sense-Giving and Sense-Reading"** (1967) — Philosophy, Vol. 42
- **"Life's Irreducible Structure"** (1968) — Science, Vol. 160
- **"Transcendence and Self-Transcendence"** (1970) — Soundings, Vol. 53
