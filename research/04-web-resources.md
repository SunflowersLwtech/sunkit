## 3. David Autor's Work on Polanyi's Paradox

### The Foundational Paper
- **"Polanyi's Paradox and the Shape of Employment Growth"** -- David Autor, 2014
  - NBER Working Paper No. 20485
  - NBER page: https://www.nber.org/papers/w20485
  - PDF: https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20485/w20485.pdf
  - SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2496241
  - MIT Shaping the Future of Work: https://shapingwork.mit.edu/research/polanyis-paradox-and-the-shape-of-employment-growth/
  - Argues that labor market polarization is a manifestation of Polanyi's paradox. Identifies two stubborn categories: abstract tasks (creativity, problem-solving) and manual tasks (situational adaptability, in-person interaction).

### Video/Audio of Autor on Polanyi
- **Faculti video lecture**: https://faculti.net/polanyis-paradox-and-the-shape-of-employment-growth
  - David Autor discusses the paper on video.
- **EconTalk podcast (Oct 6, 2014)**: https://www.econtalk.org/david-autor-on-the-future-of-work-and-polanyis-paradox/
  - Discussion with host Russ Roberts about automation, smart machines, and tacit knowledge.

## 4. Academic Papers & Articles (2020-2026): Polanyi, Tacit Knowledge & AI

### High-Impact Papers

- **Kambhampati, Subbarao. "Polanyi's Revenge and AI's New Romance with Tacit Knowledge."** Communications of the ACM, Vol. 64, pp. 31-32, February 2021.
  - https://cacm.acm.org/opinion/polanyis-revenge-and-ais-new-romance-with-tacit-knowledge/
  - PDF: https://rakaposhi.eas.asu.edu/Polanyis-Revenge-CACM-Print.pdf
  - Argues Polanyi's paradox is turning into "Polanyi's revenge" -- AI's insistence on learning everything from data ignores available explicit knowledge.

- **Lu, Janna. "Tacit Knowledge in Large Language Models."** The Review of Austrian Economics, GMU Working Paper No. 25-25, June 2025 (revised Oct 2025).
  - SSRN: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5320627
  - Springer: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11138-025-00710-5
  - Argues LLMs possess 2 of 3 forms of tacit knowledge (costly-to-codify knowledge, nuance/subtext knowledge), but not embodied knowledge.

- **"What Do Large Language Models Know? Tacit Knowledge as a Potential Causal-Explanatory Structure."** Philosophy of Science (Cambridge Core).
  - https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy-of-science/article/what-do-large-language-models-know-tacit-knowledge-as-a-potential-causalexplanatory-structure/9475F1504081116099098C37D6F57611

- **"Leveraging Large Language Models for Tacit Knowledge Discovery in Organizational Contexts."** arXiv, 2025.
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03811
  - LLM-based framework to extract, structure, and categorize tacit knowledge across teams.

- **"Revealing the Unspoken: Using LLMs to Mobilize and Enrich Tacit Knowledge in Event Logs."** AMCIS 2025 Proceedings.
  - https://aisel.aisnet.org/amcis2025/sig_svc/sig_svc/11/

- **Piore, Michael J. "Tacit Knowledge and the Future of Work Debate."** MIT Economics, 2025.

- **Sourati & Evans. "Beyond the Polanyi Paradox: How Human-Aware AI Unlocks Creativity and Breakthrough Discoveries."** Nature Human Behaviour (covered in Foley & Lardner analysis, 2025).

## 5. Knowledge Management: SECI Model & GenAI Extensions

### SECI Model Foundations
- **SECI model -- Wikipedia**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SECI_model_of_knowledge_dimensions
  - Four modes of knowledge conversion: Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization. Developed by Ikujiro Nonaka (1990), refined with Hirotaka Takeuchi (1995).
- **Nonaka & Takeuchi (2000) paper**: https://agileconsortium.pbworks.com/f/Nonaka_etal_2000_SECI.pdf
  - "SECI, Ba and Leadership: A Unified Model of Dynamic Knowledge Creation"
- **KM Insider guide to SECI**: https://kminsider.com/topic/essential-seci-model-for-effective-km/
- **ServiceNow Knowledge Creation Series**: https://www.servicenow.com/community/knowledge-management-blog/the-knowledge-creation-series-episode-1-the-seci-model-four/ba-p/3349445

### GenAI-Updated SECI Models (2025-2026)

- **"Tacit Knowledge Management with Generative AI: Proposal of the GenAI SECI Model."** arXiv, 2026.
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21866
  - Introduces "Digital Fragmented Knowledge" integrating explicit and tacit knowledge in cyberspace.

- **"Knowledge Management in the Age of Generative AI -- from SECI to GRAI."** VINE Journal of Information and Knowledge Management Systems (Emerald), 2025.
  - https://doi.org/10.1108/vjikms-10-2024-0357
  - The GRAI framework: maintains SECI cycle but adds 8 human-machine interaction fields.
  - RealKM overview: https://realkm.com/2025/05/21/the-grai-framework-extending-the-seci-model-to-reflect-generative-ai/

- **"Revisiting the SECI Model: The Impact of Generative AI on Organizational Knowledge Creation."** Springer Nature, 2025.
  - https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-14721-9_6

- **"Artificial Knowledge Generation: Investigating the Revolutionary Role of Generative AI in Knowledge Management."** Journal of Innovation & Knowledge (Elsevier), 2025.
  - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2444569X25002112

- **"Generative AI-Driven Knowledge Management in Manufacturing Firms."** Journal of Knowledge Management (Emerald), 2025.
  - https://www.emerald.com/jkm/article-abstract/doi/10.1108/JKM-03-2025-0418/
